Everything you need to know about competition format, eligibility, weight classes and equipment for Copenhagen Box Cup 2027.

COMPETITION
FORMAT

Copenhagen Box Cup brings together boxers from different clubs and countries for international competition in Copenhagen.

ELITE ELIGIBILITY

Born from 1 January 1986 to 31 December 2007. Entries must be submitted by the athlete's National Federation.

COMPETITION FORMAT

Current World Boxing Competition Rules apply. Maximum four boxers per weight category; one competition ring. Semifinals on 5 February; third-place bouts and finals on 6 February. Round duration is not specified in the invitation.

OFFICIALS & SCORING

World Boxing certified technical officials. Scoring and timing: Boxerbase.

WEIGHT
CLASSES

Eight Elite men's and four Elite women's weight classes, as listed in the event invitation.

MEN

60 KG65 KG70 KG75 KG80 KG85 KG90 KG+90 KG

WOMEN

54 KG57 KG65 KG70 KG

WEIGH-IN
& MEDICAL

All competitors must complete the required weigh-in and medical control before competing. Daily weigh-in and medical control take place on 5 and 6 February, 08:00–09:00 at Bella Sky Hotel.

REQUIREMENTS

  • ✓Passport copy
  • ✓Record book with annual medical examination within the previous 12 months
  • ✓Signed Declaration of Non-Pregnancy for women boxers
  • ✓Gender-test certificate for men and women over 18, as required by the invitation
  • ✓Red and blue competition uniforms

WEIGH-IN RULES

Weigh-in and medical: 08:00–09:00 on 5 and 6 February at Bella Sky Hotel. Current World Boxing Competition Rules apply.

MEDICAL CONTROL

The LOC provides first aid for sports injuries during the event. Participants must hold their own medical and repatriation insurance. Anti-doping controls follow World Boxing Anti-Doping Rules and the World Anti-Doping Code.

EQUIPMENT

All equipment must comply with the official competition regulations.

◯

GLOVES

Sting competition gloves are provided by the LOC.

⌒

HEADGUARDS

Women boxers must bring their own competition headguards compliant with World Boxing Competition Rules.

◎

HAND WRAPS

All boxers must use compliant Velcro bandages. The LOC does not provide bandages.

◇

COMPETITION UNIFORM

Bring red and blue uniforms compliant with World Boxing Competition Rules.

COMPETITION RULES
& MEDIA POLICY

By participating, team members agree to the image-rights terms in the invitation for World Boxing and the LOC. Recording bouts for broadcasting, including social media, is prohibited. National Federations may record their own boxers for technical purposes only, with one camera per ring in the designated area and a signed Video Recording Disclaimer submitted at the Technical Meeting.
