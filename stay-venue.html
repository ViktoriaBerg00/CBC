AC HOTEL BELLA SKY COPENHAGEN

Bella Sky Hotel is the official event hotel. Team bookings and rooming lists must be submitted to the LOC using the Hotel Booking Form at cbc@dabu.dk by 16 December 2026. Booking through the LOC is recommended but not obligatory. Contact the LOC for booking forms, current rates and payment instructions.

Hotel AC Hotel Bella Sky Copenhagen
Address Martha Christensens Vej 10, Entrance 3, 2300 København
OFFICIAL TEAM TRANSFERS

The LOC provides airport and hotel-to-venue transfers during the event only for days when teams stay in an official hotel booked through the LOC. Independently booked stays, including at Bella Sky, do not qualify.

ARRIVAL & DEPARTURE

Designated arrival points: Copenhagen Airport (CPH) and Copenhagen Central Station. Email the Transport Request Form with arrival and departure details to cbc@dabu.dk by 16 December 2026.

HOTEL & TRANSPORT CONTACT

Contact the LOC at cbc@dabu.dk or +45 32 44 41 41. Transfers outside event days incur additional charges, quoted on request. Hotel check-in: 15:00; checkout: 11:00. No training or drying clothes in hotel corridors.

TO THE HOTEL BY METRO

Take metro M1 towards Vestamager and get off at Bella Center. From the station, walk to AC Hotel Bella Sky Copenhagen at Martha Christensens Vej 10, Entrance 3.

TO THE HOTEL BY BUS

Bus 68 connects Copenhagen Central Station (Hovedbanegården, Tivoli) with the Bella Center area. Get off at Bella Center, Indgang Vest, and walk to the hotel.

AIRPORT TO HOTEL

From Copenhagen Airport, take metro M2 towards Vanløse. Change at Christianshavn to M1 towards Vestamager and get off at Bella Center. Walk from the station to the hotel.
