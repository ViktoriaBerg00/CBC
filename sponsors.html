DANISH BOXING

DaBU

Danmarks Bokse-Union

DaBU is Denmark's national boxing federation and the host federation for Copenhagen Box Cup 2027. Its work includes supporting boxing clubs, developing talent and elite boxing, and providing education for the people involved in the sport.

COPENHAGEN BOXING

IK99

Bokseklubben IK99

IK99 is a Copenhagen boxing club offering both competitive boxing and fitness training. The club brings boxers together through training and events, including Fight Night and the Copenhagen Boxing Youth Cup at Idrætshuset on Østerbro.

DANISH SPORT

DIF

Danmarks Idrætsforbund

DIF works to strengthen sport and sporting communities in Denmark. Through its work with sports federations and clubs, it supports participation, volunteering and the development of Danish sport, with a focus on fair play and the conditions that help sport thrive.
