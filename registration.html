HOW TO
REGISTER

Follow the steps below to submit your team's registration for Copenhagen Box Cup 2027.

01

REGISTER YOUR TEAM

National Federations must email preliminary entries using the entry form to cbc@dabu.dk by 16 December 2026.

02

SUBMIT REQUIRED INFORMATION

Accepted entries must complete final online registration by 16 December 2026. Contact the LOC for the registration link and forms.

03

RECEIVE CONFIRMATION

Attend the mandatory Boxing Entries Check on 4 February 2027, 15:00–19:00 at Bella Sky Hotel. Accommodation costs must be settled with the LOC before accreditation.

CONTACT ENTRIES TEAM REGISTRATION FORM
WHO CAN ENTER?

Athletes must be submitted and registered by their respective National Federation.

AGE CATEGORIES

Elite boxers born from 1 January 1986 to 31 December 2007, as specified in the invitation.

NUMBER OF ENTRIES

Maximum four boxers per weight category. Eight men's and four women's Elite weight classes.

INTERNATIONAL TEAMS

Email visa information forms and arrival/departure forms to cbc@dabu.dk by 16 December 2026. The LOC assists with visa support letters but cannot guarantee visas. The invitation requires passports valid for six months from arrival with blank pages.

BEFORE
YOU ARRIVE

Make sure your team has all required documentation and competition information ready before arriving in Copenhagen.

01

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Bring a passport copy; a boxing record book with an annual medical examination completed within the previous 12 months (national record books accepted); a signed Declaration of Non-Pregnancy for women; red and blue uniforms; and the gender-test certificate required by the invitation for men and women over 18.

02

BOXING ENTRIES CHECK

4 February 2027, 15:00–19:00 at Bella Sky Hotel. Maximum two representatives per team. Missing the scheduled appointment excludes the entire team.

03

COMPETITION EQUIPMENT

Bring red and blue uniforms and compliant Velcro bandages. Women must bring compliant competition headguards. The LOC supplies Sting gloves.

CANCELLATIONS
& REFUNDS

Withdrawals after the final entry deadline are not refunded. All accredited participants must arrange their own travel, medical, repatriation and liability insurance for the event and travel period. Contact the LOC for hotel cancellation terms and payment instructions.
